# ADR-005: Non-custodial design with PayPal operation allow-list

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-27
- Related IDs: INV-07, INV-08

## Context

Documented in ProofReturn SSOT v1.2.1 (Section 46.2) to govern system architecture, mechanism integrity, and compliance for `INV-07, INV-08`.

## Decision

Non-custodial design with PayPal operation allow-list

## Alternatives considered

Evaluated against alternative architectural and mechanism options during SSOT v1.0, v1.1, v1.2, and v1.2.1 audits (see Appendices E and F).

## Consequences (positive, negative, follow-ups)

- **Key reason / positive impact**: AUP compliance
- **Traceability**: Enforced via `INV-07, INV-08` and verified at release gates (Section 27).
